About

FAQ

Common questions

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly small and medium-sized businesses that run, or want to run, paid advertising online. That includes e-commerce, service businesses, and B2B companies. If you are not sure whether your situation is a fit, just ask.

Do I need to commit to a long-term contract?

No. I offer both monthly management and fixed-scope projects such as an account audit or a campaign launch. Monthly work is reviewed regularly and can be paused or adjusted as your needs change.

Which platforms do you manage?

Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Ads, and TikTok Ads, along with Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager for measurement. Which channels make sense depends on your audience and goals.

How does reporting work?

You receive a regular report in plain language covering spend, results, what changed, and what is planned next. I am also available for a call to walk through the numbers whenever it is useful.

How do we get started?

Send a short message using the form below or by email. I will reply to arrange an intro call, and after that you will receive a written proposal with scope and pricing before anything begins.
